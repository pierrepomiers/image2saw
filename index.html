image2saw: pixels to soundscape patterns.

It turns an image into a seamless sound loop. Every pixel of the selected area is a voice. A soft window travels across the area and makes the pixels under it sing.

how to

  1. Load an image with [ charger ], or pick one of the three examples.
  2. Drag on the image to select the area to play. Drag inside the frame to move it, drag a corner to resize it.
  3. Press [ lecture ] to listen. The space bar does the same.
  4. Change the settings. The sound is recomputed after each change, and the bar under the image shows progress. When a loop takes long to compute, a rough preview plays first and the full-quality loop replaces it.
  5. [ wav ] exports the loop as a 16-bit stereo file. [ vidéo ] records one loop of the moving image with its sound.

what the image does

hue
Sets the pitch: red is low, violet is high. In a black and white image, brightness sets the pitch.
brightness
Sets the loudness of each voice.
saturation
Sets how long each voice rings.

settings

caméra
Plays the live picture of the phone or computer camera instead of a still image: move the camera and the sound follows at once. [ retourner ] switches between the back and front cameras. [ figer ] keeps the current picture and goes back to the normal mode, where loops can be exported. Picking a demo image or loading one also leaves the camera. The live sound is held at the same loudness as the frozen loop. The camera only works when the page is opened in a browser such as Safari or Chrome, not inside Claude.
zoom
With the camera, zooms in up to 8×. The lens zoom of the device is used when the browser gives access to it; beyond that, or without it, the picture is cropped around its centre.
images/s
With the camera, how many times per second the sound and the picture are refreshed, from 1 to 30. Low values give a stepped, stroboscopic result.
résolution
Number of pixels on the long side of the area, from 1 to 64. Every pixel is visited, so more pixels means faster travel (see ms/px under the image) and a longer computation. With the camera it is capped at 32.
parcours
Path of the window. Step through the five paths with < and >. h-snake and v-snake snake through every pixel once, by rows or by columns. aller-retour goes there and back. spirale winds to the centre and back out. chaos wanders at random from one pixel to a neighbour, still crossing every pixel once before it loops; [ autre ] draws another random path.
largeur
Width of the window, as a share of the area. Around 50 %, nearly every pixel sings at once.
résonance
How long a voice keeps ringing after the window has passed.
influence
How much the image drives width and resonance. Flat areas widen the window, busy areas narrow it, vivid colours ring longer.
onde
Waveform of every voice: sine, triangle, sawtooth or square.
grave, aigu
Lowest and highest pitch. The sliders move by semitones from C0 to B6, and the note name is shown next to the value. To set any other frequency, type it in hertz: for example 5 for rumbles and beats.
échelle
Linear or logarithmic spread of the pitches between those two limits.
accord
Leaves pitches free, or snaps them to the chromatic or pentatonic scale.
death
Output distortion, from 0 (off) to 12. The deep bass stays clean; everything above it is driven through a tube-style saturation, then from 6 upwards through a stage that folds the wave over itself, then a speaker-like filter and a heavy compressor. The higher the value, the thicker and more torn the sound. It comes before the equaliser, and the overall level stays under control. The line turns red and shakes near the top.
égaliseur
Seven output faders, one per octave, each from −12 to +12 dB with a mark every 6 dB. The first one sets everything below 30 Hz. The last one sets everything above 2 kHz. The sliders stop at B6, just below, so it mostly shapes the harmonics that make the saw and square waves bright; only a pitch typed by hand can reach higher. The overall level is kept steady and never clips: cutting the lowest bands trades inaudible rumble for more level in the rest. [ plat ] sets all bands back to 0 dB.
stéréo
Stereo width. The left of the image plays in the left speaker.
mesure, mesures, tempo
Loop length in bars, so that samples line up on the beat: a slower tempo makes a longer loop, shown in seconds under the image. Choose libre to set a length in seconds with durée.
wobble
Strength of the ripple on the image while it plays, up to 200 %. The ripple follows the real loudness and pitch of the sound.
sauver, ouvrir, défauts
Save the settings to a .json file, load one back, or return to the starting values.

good to know

  • Loops are seamless: the end joins the start with no click.
  • Type a value next to a slider to set it exactly. Double-click a name to reset it.
  • Beyond the edges, the image is mirrored, so the window is never half empty.
  • Video is recorded in real time: keep the page visible until it ends.
  • On an iPhone, the silent switch mutes the sound.
